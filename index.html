Argentina. Buenos Aires

Flag oficial of the Province of Buenos Aires

Buenos Aires is the capital of Argentina and one of the most important cities in South America. It is known for its architecture, culture, restaurants, and many historic places. It is famous for tango, soccer, and the passion of its people for this sport. An interesting fact is that Buenos Aires is known as the birthplace of tango, a musical genre and dance that originated in the Río de la Plata region and is now famous around the world.
